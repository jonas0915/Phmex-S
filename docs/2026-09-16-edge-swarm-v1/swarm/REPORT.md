# Strategy search — final report (2026-09-16)

## 1. Verdict

Zero of 5 candidates passed. All 5 were refuted by at least one reviewer (4 of 5 by both reviewers), none survived vetting, and no test/audit run was performed on any of them (the test+audit input for this task is empty — nothing reached that stage).

Nothing here is worth a paper slot. Every candidate either re-treads a mechanism this account's own record already killed (S/R zones, liquidation-cascade/vol-fade reversion, OFI-imbalance reversion, ST2.0's un-calibrated OFI-rollover gate, 1h vol-expansion), or is backtest-free "reasoning estimate" math that, even taken at face value, needs a breakeven win rate the mechanism has no evidence of clearing (71-78% on two of them) or a sample size measured in months-to-years at the claimed trade frequency.

Recommendation: ship no new paper slot from this batch. Append the 5 one-line entries in Section 3 to the dead list. Do not re-mine any of these five mechanisms without a genuinely new angle and an actual (not reasoning-only) backtest.

## 3. FAIL / refuted candidates — dead-list entries

- **Prior-Day Value-Area Edge Breakout (vae_bo)** — refuted (1 of 2 reviewers; medium/high confidence): the mechanism is genuinely untested territory per the dead list's own S/R-family note, but it ships with no backtest and no pre-registered DOA line — a precondition the dead list explicitly requires for retrying this family (01_dead_list.md:191, citing reference_sr_bounce_lever_lab_2026-07-29.md:61-63) — and at its own claimed 5-8 trades/week it would take roughly a year-plus to reach the 95% CI pass bar even at a favorable assumed win rate.
- **1h Bollinger-Squeeze Breakout (bb_squeeze_breakout)** — refuted (2 of 2): nearest analog on this exact data source, 1h vol-expansion, already died from single-split selection bias (01_dead_list.md row 3: OOS +0.26% one split vs full-sample −0.187%/trade, Sharpe −2.45); this candidate is backtest-only, models no slippage/drift on its market-order chase-the-breakout entry, and its partial-TP structure is explicitly designed to inflate the visible TP-hit rate rather than net expectancy — the same cosmetic the dead list already flags (avg win $0.46 vs avg loss −$0.85, 01_dead_list.md:231).
- **S/R Rejection Reversion — Taker-Chase Entry (sr_rejection_taker)** — refuted (2 of 2, one high confidence): reuses the exact zone-construction method (3-bar fractal pivots) of the killed S/R-bounce family (01_dead_list.md #25: 5,084-trade holdout gross-negative before fees, TP-hit ~29% vs 32.8% breakeven, live paper killed n=50 at −$0.79); switching to a taker-chase entry adds ~12bps cost and forces every signal into the trade population that SR_BOUNCE's own I3 fill-revalidation showed underperforms the (maker) non-fills it would otherwise have skipped.
- **Large-Print Tape Snap-Back (tape_snapback)** — refuted (2 of 2, both high confidence): economically the same bet as the killed liquidation-cascade-reversion family (01_dead_list.md #6: "high-vol bars CONTINUE, don't revert"), just detected via tick-level flow instead of bar volume; separately, its own fee math misapplies the maker-blend 0.07% rate (03_exchange_economics.md §2/§5) to what it describes as a taker entry, understating true round-trip cost by roughly half and implying a required win rate above ~71%.
- **OFI-Rollover Reversion Entry (ofi_rollover)** — refuted (2 of 2, both high confidence): "wait-for-OFI-rollover" is the exact entry gate the record already parked on ST2.0 and never calibrated before that book's demotion (01_dead_list.md:195); the candidate's own frequency probe shows the raw sign-flip trigger fires 75x/day and is, in its own words, "noise-dominated"; after the verified taker-taker fee (0.12% of notional, 03_exchange_economics.md §2/§5) plus the record's documented ~4.5bps adverse-selection drift, the stated TP/SL geometry needs roughly a 78% win rate to break even.

## 4. Honest limits

- None of the 5 candidates had an actual backtest, replay, or paper run executed against local data this session — every rejection above is the candidate's own stated mechanism and numbers checked against the existing research record and the verified fee/size table (03_exchange_economics.md), not a fresh empirical test. A candidate being refuted here means it failed on arithmetic and precedent, not that fresh data was run and came back negative.
- Local data cannot test funding-based mechanisms at all right now: one reviewer confirmed zero funding-rate history exists anywhere on disk (backtest_data*, flow_capture.jsonl, logs/l2_ticks all lack a funding field), which is why the funding-extremity idea was dropped before vetting even started. Any future funding-based idea needs ≥30 days of freshly collected `fetch_funding_rate_history` data first.
- No local OHLCV exists for Phemex's coin-margined inverse contracts, and this session's exchange probe (03_exchange_economics.md) only checked fee/lot/depth data for the 10 USDT-linear symbols already in scanner scope — inverse-perp fee rates are unverified, and ARB/INJ (named in ofi_rollover) were never probed either, so their tradability at $87.12 is unknown.
- Per the hard-rules trap list, the live bot fires on the forming candle — a closed-bar replay of any of these candidates (had one been run) would test the wrong population versus what actually trades live. The only reliable adjudicator for a candidate that clears vetting is a pre-registered forward/paper run, not a backtest; none of these 5 got far enough to need one.
- Context beyond this batch: a separate literature-scan document in this scratchpad (04_edge_candidates.md, compiled 9/14) modeled breakeven win rates by holding period at this account's real cost (≈11.5bps round trip, its §4.2) — 73% needed for a 25bps intraday scalp vs ~52% for a 300bps multi-day hold — and concluded every documented retail-survivable edge (unlock shorts, post-listing fades, trend) sits at the multi-day-or-longer horizon, while every intraday mechanism in this account's own dead list has failed. None of the 5 candidates vetted in this task explored that multi-day/event-driven space the owner's scope explicitly allows (multi-day/weekly holds, other Phemex instruments); all 5 were intraday mean-reversion or breakout variants. That same document's own dollar-ceiling math (§4.3-4.4) notes even a genuine 20%/yr net edge nets roughly $17/yr on $87.12, and flags Phemex's 5% APY USDT Flexible Savings as the only [FOUND], no-forward-test-required positive-expectancy action at this balance — cited here as existing research context, not a recommendation to act on.
- All figures in Section 3 are transcribed from the vetting reviewers' own citations back to 01_dead_list.md and 03_exchange_economics.md; this report did not independently re-verify every cent, but every number quoted traces to a specific line in one of those two files as shown.
